How Your Biometric Privacy is Protected

Your raw biometric data — fingerprint scan or Face ID geometry — never leaves your device. The biometric scanner unlocks the private key inside your device's hardware Secure Enclave. Only an asymmetric cryptographic signature is transmitted to the CyberNova server. The server holds only your public key and verifies the signature — it cannot reconstruct your biometric.
